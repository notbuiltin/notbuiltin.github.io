Account & Data Deletion

Last updated: July 13, 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your PhoneLimit account and all associated data. The app is published on Google Play under the package name app.phonelimit.

How to request deletion

PhoneLimit uses Firebase Authentication and Firestore for its friends feature. To permanently delete your account and the data linked to it, send us an email:

Send a deletion request
  1. Email notbuiltin@gmail.com
  2. Use the subject line “Delete my PhoneLimit account”
  3. Include the email address you used inside the app (for the friends / family mode feature) so we can locate your data
Email a deletion request

We process deletion requests within 30 days and will confirm by email once completed.

What gets deleted

  • Your Firebase Authentication account (the email/anonymous identity created by the app)
  • Your Firestore profile (display name, weekly average screen time, category breakdown, streak, and shared app list)
  • Your friendship connections and any invite codes you generated

What stays on your device

Some data never leaves your phone and is therefore not stored on our servers. It is removed automatically when you uninstall the app:

  • Detailed app usage statistics and screen time history
  • The app time limits you set
  • Family mode pattern lock and backup PIN (stored only as SHA-256 hashes)
  • Streak calendar history

Questions

For anything related to your data, contact notbuiltin@gmail.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
